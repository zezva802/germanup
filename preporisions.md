Two-way prepositions (9)

auf — object ON top of something
unter — object UNDER something
neben — object NEXT TO something
vor — object IN FRONT of something
hinter — object BEHIND something
an — object ON the side/wall (like a picture hanging on a wall)
zwischen — object BETWEEN two things
über — object ABOVE something (not touching)
in — object INSIDE something

durch — person/object going THROUGH something (tunnel, door)
um — person/object going AROUND something (tree, corner)
gegen — object hitting/leaning AGAINST something (ball against wall)

aus — person/object coming OUT OF something (house, box)
zu — person walking TOWARD something (door, building)
gegenüber — two things FACING each other (chairs across a table)
bei — person standing AT/NEAR a place (next to a shop, at someone's house)
